# Method provenance and further reading

These links are reading suggestions, not findings established by this file.
- Before you use one to substantiate a research or legal statement, verify its source, context, date and exact conclusion.
- The skill's method is self-contained and makes no empirical conversion or compliance guarantee.

## Keep different kinds of authority separate

- The scoped factual owner supplies offer-specific evidence.
- Competent decision authority accepts positioning choices.
- Research can inform general hypotheses once assessed; it does not establish what a particular offer does.
- Official sources need the appropriate specialist's analysis of jurisdiction and intended use before a legal conclusion.
- None of them grants access, acceptance or publication authority.

## Research links

- Packard and Berger (2021), *How Concrete Language Shapes Customer Satisfaction*, Journal of Consumer Research: [publisher page](https://academic.oup.com/jcr/article/47/5/787/5873524). On concrete language; infer no conversion guarantee from it.
- Amershi and colleagues (2019), *Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction*, CHI: [authors' publication page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/). On communicating system behavior and controls.
- Lee and See (2004), *Trust in Automation*, Human Factors: [publisher page](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1518/hfes.46.1.50_30392). On reliance and trust language.
- Buçinca, Malaya and Gajos (2021), *To Trust or to Think*, CSCW: [publisher page](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3449287). On AI decision support and reliance.
- Bansal and colleagues (2021), *Does the Whole Exceed its Parts?*, CHI: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.14779). On explanations and human-AI decisions.

## Official source links

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework): a risk-review reference, not a certification for any offer.
- [FTC advertising substantiation policy statement](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/ftc-policy-statement-regarding-advertising-substantiation): a source for specialist assessment of applicable advertising rules, not a universal legal conclusion.
- [EU AI Act official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj): a source for specialist assessment of intended use, applicable rules and dates. The skill's sensitive-use tripwires are escalation heuristics, not legal classifications.
