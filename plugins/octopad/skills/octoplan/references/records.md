# Delivery records

Tasks are the Plan: specifications, dependencies, routes, verification, progress and task evidence. The tracker projects follow-up. Neither is copied into a delivery page.

## Working documents, one home for each record

- **Contract:** only shared confirmed intent and its source, stakes (decision served, blast radius, reversibility, countable success, kill question and actual user limits), outcome proof, boundaries, constraints, effects, access, checkpoints and gates missing from their existing homes. Reference task specifications and effective rules instead of copying them. Omit this document when the genuine owning task already holds the complete frame.
- **Journal:** a small current-supervision header, followed by control receipts: exact consent, source, time, mode and bounds; plan reviews and dispositions; activation; changes, revocations, gate continuations, ownership transfers and closure. The header holds one entry per disjoint boundary it covers, naming the boundary, current actor, exact route, native continuity reference if used, and active, released or closed state. Receipts preserve their predecessors. Append-only is an agent rule, not server enforcement.

Use working pages of a real owning task or active stream, in an ordinary folder. An ongoing stream can own temporary documents for a bounded delivery; name its outcome, covered tasks and finish line in the records. Never invent a task or stream just to host them. The owner must remain open while its documents serve active work; shared work uses the genuine integration/outcome task or an owning stream that remains active for that boundary.

Before creation or takeover, read existing working-document pointers, current owners and in-flight work. Reuse one canonical journal for the same boundary. Different document or mandate identities never justify overlapping active ownership. Link the journal and any contract from covered tasks, preserving existing page links; their owner also lists them as working pages. Record each document's workspace and identity in the journal. Across workspaces, read through the owning workspace with actual access; do not invent cross-workspace page links. Verify the working mark and owner after creation. Missing capability pauses affected work; no ordinary-page or Key Information fallback grants it or missing authority.

Task execution receipts stay once on their owning task in compact comments; long reports and worker answers use its working pages. The journal links required task evidence rather than copying it. Keep no polls, progress board, task specifications or house-rule history there. Genuine lasting choices, facts, questions and risks still use their proper knowledge homes; do not create automatic Brief, Stakes, Plan-contract, delivery-authorization or supervisor Decisions.

## Guard control changes and preserve review binding

Read the current journal before dispatch, a protected effect, gate clearance or closure. Claim, release or replace its current supervision with `pages(action: "update", content_updates, expected_updated_at)` using the revision just read. Preserve all receipts. Consent, activation, revocation and gate-continuation writes also use guarded updates; ordinary evidence may append. On conflict, reread and reconcile before acting. A successor proves predecessor cessation and reconciles children and uncertain effects first. A guarded page update is not a native actor lock or proof of cessation.

Reviews name exact task identities and revisions, and the contract identity and revision, or the owning task when it supplies that frame. Keep review and activation receipts in the journal, separate from the reviewed contract. Adding a receipt or changing supervision does not change the contract revision. A changed task or contract timestamp still requires reread and evidence of unchanged material content; preserve the comparison evidence, or obtain the affected recheck when it cannot be proved. Page undo history is not permanent review evidence. A changed outcome, task meaning, dependency, route, verifier, effect, checkpoint or gate gets its applicable review before dependent work.

Keep superseded consent, reviews and transfers as history. Current scope is the latest applicable, unretracted consent within its actual bounds, never a header's assertion or an old receipt in isolation. Activation binds that authority to current PASS evidence; it cannot widen permission. Existing valid Decision-based plans keep their canonical records and guarded updates under the runtime's compatibility reader; do not create a parallel journal on resume or migrate them automatically.

## Finish the bounded delivery

After current integrated proof, reconcile actors and effects, release supervision and record closure. Extract lasting knowledge only where its normal criteria apply. Archive the contract and journal by their page IDs with `pages(action: "update", page_id, folder_id: <Archive UUID>, expected_updated_at)`; resolve that folder in their own workspace. Keep the working mark and owner so direct recovery remains possible. Preserve a document as an ordinary reference only when it actually outlives this delivery.

In an ongoing stream, archive only this mandate's documents, never all stream working pages or the stream itself. Shared records stay active until their whole boundary closes. Archive is consultable and excluded from default AI search; active working documents remain searchable. Do not promise retrieval isolation or server-sealed receipts.
